## Caveats

- **Billing lag.** Cost Management data typically lags usage by about 8–24
  hours. The 21 Oct row is partial and probably excludes most of that day's
  deployment, including run 39580927565. Earlier days may also still be
  revised.
- **Scope.** The figure covers only resources billed in `rg-tripgenie`. It is
  not a whole-subscription or monthly figure, and it should not be projected
  forward without a later query.
- **Not a forecast.** The deployment README's figure of about US$2 a day is an
  estimate, not this observation.
