# Destination guides

General trip-planning guidance for popular destinations. Opening hours, prices,
and schedules change; confirm time-sensitive details with the operator before
booking.

## Sydney: overview and best time to visit

Sydney is Australia's largest city, built around a large natural harbour with
ocean beaches to the east and national parks to the north and south. Spring
(September to November) and autumn (March to May) are the most comfortable
seasons for sightseeing in Sydney. Summer (December to February) is hot and is
peak beach season, with New Year's Eve harbour fireworks drawing large crowds.
Winter (June to August) is mild and is a good time for coastal walks and whale
watching. A first visit to Sydney usually needs three to five days.

## Sydney: popular activities

- See the Sydney Opera House, take a guided tour, or attend a performance.
- Walk across or climb the Sydney Harbour Bridge.
- Take the ferry from Circular Quay to Manly for harbour views.
- Walk the Bondi to Coogee coastal walk (about 6 km, allow two to three hours).
- Explore The Rocks, the historic quarter beside Circular Quay.
- Visit the Royal Botanic Garden and Mrs Macquarie's Chair lookout.
- Visit Taronga Zoo, reached by ferry across the harbour.
- Swim or surf at Bondi, Manly, or Coogee beaches, between the red and yellow
  flags.

## Sydney: getting around and day trips

Sydney's trains, buses, ferries, and light rail use the Opal card, and
contactless bank cards can also be tapped on and off. Ferries are both
transport and sightseeing. The Blue Mountains (Katoomba and the Three Sisters)
are about two hours west of Sydney by train or car and make a full day trip.
The Hunter Valley wine region is about two and a half hours north of Sydney,
and the Royal National Park is about an hour south.

Suggested three-day Sydney outline: day one around Circular Quay, the Opera
House, The Rocks, and the Botanic Garden; day two on the Bondi to Coogee walk
and a harbour ferry to Manly; day three in the Blue Mountains.

## Melbourne: overview and best time to visit

Melbourne is known for laneway cafes, food, street art, live music, and major
sporting events. Melbourne weather is changeable and locals describe it as
"four seasons in one day", so pack layers in any season. March to May (autumn)
and September to November (spring) are pleasant times to visit Melbourne. The
Australian Open tennis runs in January and the Melbourne Cup horse race is held
on the first Tuesday in November. A first visit to Melbourne usually needs
three to four days, plus extra days for regional trips.

## Melbourne: popular activities

- Explore the central laneways and street art, including Hosier Lane.
- Visit the Queen Victoria Market for produce and food stalls.
- Watch a match or take a tour at the Melbourne Cricket Ground (MCG).
- Walk the Royal Botanic Gardens and the Yarra River at Southbank.
- Visit the National Gallery of Victoria.
- Spend an afternoon at St Kilda beach, pier, and Acland Street.
- Try the city's specialty coffee and brunch culture.

## Melbourne: getting around and day trips

Melbourne trams are free within the central Free Tram Zone; beyond it, trams,
trains, and buses use the myki card or supported contactless payment. Popular
day trips from Melbourne:

- The Great Ocean Road and the Twelve Apostles: roughly three and a half to four
  hours' drive each way, so an overnight stay is more relaxed than a day trip.
- Phillip Island Penguin Parade: about two hours' drive, best timed for dusk.
- Yarra Valley wineries: about one hour's drive.
- Puffing Billy steam railway in the Dandenong Ranges: about one hour's drive.

## Tokyo: overview and best time to visit

Tokyo is Japan's capital, a very large city made up of distinct neighbourhoods
with excellent public transport. Spring in Tokyo (late March to early April)
brings cherry blossoms and is one of the busiest travel periods. Autumn
(October to November) has clear, mild weather and autumn leaves. Tokyo summers
(July and August) are hot and humid, the rainy season usually runs from June to
mid-July, and typhoons are most likely from August to October. Winter is cold,
dry, and usually sunny. A first visit to Tokyo usually needs four to six days.

## Tokyo: popular activities

- Cross the Shibuya Scramble Crossing and visit the observation decks nearby.
- Visit Senso-ji temple and Nakamise shopping street in Asakusa.
- Walk to Meiji Jingu shrine and nearby Harajuku and Omotesando.
- Explore Shinjuku, including Shinjuku Gyoen garden and the evening lanes of
  Omoide Yokocho and Golden Gai.
- Eat at the Tsukiji Outer Market.
- Visit Akihabara for electronics, anime, and games.
- See digital art at the teamLab museums.
- View the city from Tokyo Skytree or Tokyo Tower.

## Tokyo: getting around and day trips

Tokyo trains and subways are the fastest way around; the JR Yamanote loop line
connects most major districts. Rechargeable IC cards such as Suica and PASMO
work on trains, buses, and in many shops. Avoid carrying large luggage on
trains during the morning rush. Popular day trips from Tokyo:

- Hakone: hot springs, lake cruises, and Mount Fuji views, about 90 minutes
  away.
- Kamakura: temples and the Great Buddha, about one hour away.
- Nikko: ornate shrines and mountain scenery, about two hours away.

## Gold Coast: overview and best time to visit

The Gold Coast in Queensland is a coastal holiday city known for surf beaches,
theme parks, and subtropical hinterland rainforest. It is warm year-round.
Summer (December to February) is hot and humid with afternoon storms, and
school holidays make theme parks busy. April to October is drier and more
comfortable. The Gold Coast is about one hour's drive south of Brisbane and
has its own airport at Coolangatta. Three to five days suits most Gold Coast
trips, longer for families doing several theme parks.

## Gold Coast: popular activities

- Swim or learn to surf at Surfers Paradise, Burleigh Heads, or Coolangatta,
  between the flags.
- Visit theme parks such as Dreamworld, Warner Bros. Movie World, Sea World,
  and Wet'n'Wild. Multi-park passes can reduce costs for longer stays.
- Walk the Burleigh Heads headland track.
- Take in the view from the SkyPoint observation deck.
- Visit the hinterland: Springbrook National Park (Natural Bridge and glow
  worms), Lamington National Park, and Tamborine Mountain.
- Getting around: the G:link light rail runs along the coast between
  Broadbeach, Surfers Paradise, and Southport.

## Queenstown: overview and best time to visit

Queenstown, on Lake Wakatipu in New Zealand's South Island, is a year-round
adventure and scenery destination. Queenstown winter (roughly June to
October) is ski season at Coronet Peak and The Remarkables. Summer (December
to February) suits hiking, lake activities, and long daylight hours. Autumn
(March to May) brings golden colours, especially around Arrowtown. Mountain
weather in Queenstown changes quickly, so carry warm, waterproof layers. Four
to six days allows Queenstown plus a Milford Sound trip.

## Queenstown: popular activities

- Bungy jump at the Kawarau Gorge Suspension Bridge, the site of the world's
  first commercial bungy operation.
- Ride the Shotover Jet boat through the Shotover River canyons.
- Take the Skyline Gondola for views and the luge.
- Cruise Lake Wakatipu on the historic TSS Earnslaw steamship.
- Visit Arrowtown, a restored gold-mining village.
- Drive the scenic road to Glenorchy.
- Ski or snowboard at Coronet Peak or The Remarkables in winter.

## Queenstown: Milford Sound and driving

Milford Sound is the most popular excursion from Queenstown. The drive is
roughly four hours each way, so many visitors take a coach tour with a boat
cruise, stay overnight in Te Anau, or take a scenic flight. The road can close
in severe weather or avalanche risk, and winter drivers may need snow chains.
New Zealand drives on the left.

## Singapore: overview and best time to visit

Singapore is a compact island city-state known for food, gardens, modern
architecture, and a mix of Chinese, Malay, Indian, and colonial heritage.
Singapore is hot and humid all year, with frequent short thunderstorms. The
northeast monsoon from about December to early March is the wettest period.
Chinese New Year (January or February) brings festive decorations but some
businesses close. Three to four days suits a first visit, and Singapore is a
popular stopover between Australia and Europe or Asia.

## Singapore: popular activities

- See the Supertree Grove light show at Gardens by the Bay in the evening.
- Visit the Marina Bay waterfront and Merlion Park.
- Eat at hawker centres such as Maxwell Food Centre, Lau Pa Sat, or Chinatown
  Complex; Singapore's hawker culture is UNESCO-recognised.
- Explore Chinatown, Little India, and Kampong Glam.
- Visit Sentosa island for beaches and Universal Studios Singapore.
- Visit Singapore Zoo and the Night Safari.
- Walk the Singapore Botanic Gardens, a UNESCO World Heritage Site.
- See the Rain Vortex waterfall at Jewel Changi Airport.

## Singapore: getting around and local rules

Singapore's MRT trains and buses are clean, frequent, and inexpensive, and
accept contactless bank cards. Taxis and ride-hailing are widely available.
Singapore strictly enforces rules against littering, jaywalking, and eating or
drinking on MRT trains, with fines for breaches. Plan outdoor sightseeing for
mornings and evenings and indoor activities in the midday heat.

## Perth: overview and best time to visit

Perth is the capital of Western Australia, one of the most isolated major
cities in the world, known for beaches, sunsets over the Indian Ocean, and
easy access to Rottnest Island. Perth has a Mediterranean climate with hot, dry
summers and mild, wetter winters. Spring (September to November) brings
wildflowers across Western Australia and comfortable temperatures. Three to
five days suits Perth plus Fremantle and Rottnest Island.

## Perth: popular activities

- Walk Kings Park and Botanic Garden for city and river views.
- Take a ferry to Rottnest Island to cycle, snorkel, and see quokkas.
- Explore Fremantle: the markets, cafes, and the UNESCO-listed Fremantle
  Prison.
- Swim at Cottesloe or Scarborough beach and watch the sunset.
- Visit Swan Valley wineries, breweries, and farm gates.
- Stroll Elizabeth Quay on the Swan River.
- Day trip to the Pinnacles Desert in Nambung National Park, about two and a
  half hours north of Perth.

## Hobart: overview and best time to visit

Hobart is the capital of Tasmania, a small harbour city at the foot of
kunanyi/Mount Wellington, known for food, history, and the MONA art museum.
Hobart has a cool temperate climate: summer (December to February) is mild and
the most popular time to visit, while winter is cold but hosts the Dark Mofo
festival in June. Three to four days suits Hobart, and a week allows a wider
Tasmanian road trip.

## Hobart: popular activities

- Visit MONA (Museum of Old and New Art), reached by ferry up the Derwent
  River.
- Browse the Salamanca Market, held on Saturday mornings.
- Drive or take a tour to the summit of kunanyi/Mount Wellington for views;
  it can be cold and windy even in summer.
- Walk historic Battery Point and the waterfront.
- Day trip to the Port Arthur Historic Site, about 90 minutes' drive.
- Visit Bruny Island for local food, coastal scenery, and wildlife.
- Visit the historic village of Richmond and Mount Field National Park.

## Brisbane: overview and best time to visit

Brisbane is Queensland's capital, a relaxed subtropical river city and a
gateway to the Gold Coast, Sunshine Coast, and nearby islands. Brisbane's dry
season from April to October is warm and sunny. Summer is hot and humid with
afternoon storms. Two to three days suits Brisbane itself, plus extra days for
the coasts and islands.

## Brisbane: popular activities

- Visit the South Bank Parklands and swim at Streets Beach, a free
  lifeguarded lagoon.
- Hold or see koalas at Lone Pine Koala Sanctuary.
- Ride the CityCat ferries along the Brisbane River.
- Climb the Story Bridge or take in the Mount Coot-tha lookout.
- Visit the Queensland Art Gallery and Gallery of Modern Art (QAGOMA).
- Visit Moreton Island (Tangalooma) or North Stradbroke Island for beaches
  and wildlife.
- Day trips from Brisbane: the Gold Coast (about one hour), the Sunshine Coast
  and Australia Zoo (about one to one and a half hours).

## Adelaide: overview and best time to visit

Adelaide is the capital of South Australia, a walkable, festival-focused city
surrounded by some of Australia's best-known wine regions. Adelaide has a
Mediterranean climate with hot, dry summers. Late summer is festival season:
the Adelaide Fringe (February to March) and WOMADelaide (March), while the Tour
Down Under cycling race is held in January. Spring and autumn are comfortable
for wine touring. Three to five days suits Adelaide and one or two wine
regions.

## Adelaide: popular activities

- Eat and shop at the Adelaide Central Market.
- Walk North Terrace for the Art Gallery of South Australia and the South
  Australian Museum.
- Take the tram to Glenelg beach.
- Watch sport or take a roof climb at Adelaide Oval.
- Visit the Barossa Valley (about one hour away) or McLaren Vale wineries.
- Visit the German-heritage village of Hahndorf in the Adelaide Hills.
- Meet kangaroos and koalas at Cleland Wildlife Park.
- Kangaroo Island, reached by ferry from Cape Jervis, needs at least two days.
