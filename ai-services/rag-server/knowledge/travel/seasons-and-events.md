# Seasons, weather, and events calendar

Seasonal guidance for trip timing. Event dates vary from year to year; confirm
dates with the organiser before planning around them.

## Seasons in Australia and New Zealand

Australia and New Zealand are in the Southern Hemisphere, so their seasons are
the reverse of Europe, North America, and Japan. Summer is December to
February, autumn is March to May, winter is June to August, and spring is
September to November. Northern Australia is tropical with a wet season from
about November to April, while southern cities have four distinct seasons.
School holidays and the Christmas to late January summer break are the busiest
and most expensive domestic travel periods.

## Seasons in Japan

Japan is in the Northern Hemisphere. Spring (March to May) brings cherry
blossoms, usually peaking in Tokyo in late March or early April. Golden Week,
a cluster of public holidays from late April to early May, is very busy
throughout Japan. The rainy season typically runs from June to mid-July,
summers are hot and humid, and typhoons are most likely from August to October.
Autumn (October to November) is mild with colourful leaves. Winter is cold, and
snow is common in the mountains and in northern Japan.

## Climate in Singapore

Singapore is near the equator and is hot and humid all year, typically around
25 to 32 degrees Celsius. Rain can fall in any month, usually as short, heavy
afternoon thunderstorms. The northeast monsoon from about December to early
March is the wettest period. Plan outdoor sightseeing in the morning or
evening, and carry an umbrella.

## Sun, heat, and outdoor safety

The UV index in Australia and New Zealand is high, especially from spring to
autumn. Wear sunscreen, a hat, and sunglasses, and seek shade in the middle of
the day. At Australian beaches, swim between the red and yellow flags, where
lifeguards patrol. In summer, check bushfire warnings before hiking or driving
through national parks. In alpine areas such as Queenstown and Tasmania,
weather changes quickly, so carry warm and waterproof layers.

## Events calendar: January to April

- January: Australian Open tennis (Melbourne); Tour Down Under cycling
  (Adelaide); Sydney Festival.
- January or February: Lunar New Year celebrations (Singapore, Sydney,
  Melbourne); some Singapore businesses close for several days.
- February to March: Adelaide Fringe arts festival.
- March: WOMADelaide music festival (Adelaide); Melbourne Food and Wine
  Festival.
- Late March to early April: cherry blossom season in Tokyo.
- March or April: Easter long weekend, a busy domestic travel period in
  Australia and New Zealand.

## Events calendar: May to August

- Late April to early May: Golden Week holidays across Japan.
- Late May to mid-June: Vivid Sydney light festival.
- June: Dark Mofo winter festival (Hobart).
- June to October: ski season in Queenstown and the New Zealand Southern Alps.
- June to November: whale-watching season along the east coast of Australia.

## Events calendar: September to December

- September to November: wildflower season in Western Australia.
- September or October: Singapore Formula One Grand Prix night race.
- Late September: AFL Grand Final (Melbourne).
- First Tuesday in November: Melbourne Cup horse race.
- December 31: New Year's Eve fireworks on Sydney Harbour; vantage points fill
  early.
- Late December to early January: Taste of Tasmania food festival (Hobart).
