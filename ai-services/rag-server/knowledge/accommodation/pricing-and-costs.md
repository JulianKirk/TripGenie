# Accommodation pricing and trip costs

How TripGenie prices accommodation and how a stay adds to a trip's committed
cost. Accommodation prices are planning figures, never amounts charged.

## Accommodation price: price per night

Each listing has one price per night. It is the price for the whole listing
per night, not per guest, so it does not change with the number of
travellers. Prices are shown with two decimal places.

## Accommodation price: currency

Accommodation prices and accommodation trip costs are reported in Australian
dollars (AUD). A stay's cost is reported in the same currency as the listing
price, so no exchange rate is applied when it is added to a trip.

## Accommodation price: filtering by price

A search can set a minimum and a maximum price per night. The filter
compares the nightly price, not the cost of a whole stay, so a cheap nightly
rate can still be the most expensive choice for a long stay.

## Accommodation costs: the cost of one stay

The cost of a stay is the price per night multiplied by the number of nights,
where the nights are the days between the check-in date and the check-out
date. A stay saved without a check-out date counts as one night. For
example, a listing at 120.00 AUD per night booked from 3 March to 6 March is
three nights and costs 360.00 AUD.

## Accommodation costs: a trip's committed accommodation cost

A trip's committed accommodation cost adds up the cost of every stay on that
trip. It lists each stay with the listing name and its amount, followed by
the total and the currency. The TripGenie budget feature reads this total as
the trip's committed accommodation spending, so the budget and the
accommodation page agree on the figure.

## Accommodation costs: how the budget uses accommodation costs

The TripGenie budget feature reads a trip's committed accommodation cost from
the accommodation feature, with its currency, and counts it as committed
accommodation spending against the trip's budget. Because the figure is
worked out from the stays currently on the trip, adding or removing a stay
changes what the budget shows the next time it reads the trip. The amounts
remain planning estimates for the budget, not payments made to a property.
